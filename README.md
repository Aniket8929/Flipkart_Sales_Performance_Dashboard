# 🏢 Business Case Study – Flipkart Sales Analytics Dashboard

## Business Scenario

Imagine an e-commerce business like Flipkart that handles a large number of orders, products, and customers.

The management team wants to understand overall business performance but needs a single place to explore sales, order trends, product performance, and customer activity.

To address this need, I developed an interactive Sales Analytics Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor overall business performance.
- Analyze sales trends and performance.
- Understand order-related metrics.
- Identify high-performing products.
- Analyze customer purchasing behaviour.
- Explore detailed product information.

## 👥 Key Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Business Requirement |
|---|---|
| Business Management | Monitor overall performance |
| Sales Manager | Track sales performance |
| Operations Manager | Analyze order performance |
| Product Manager | Evaluate product performance |
| Marketing Team | Understand customer behaviour |

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- Power Query – Data Cleaning and Transformation
- DAX – Business Calculations and Measures
- Data Modelling – Table Relationships
- Interactive Visualizations
- Drillthrough and Report Navigation

## 📈 Business Value

This dashboard provides a centralized view of e-commerce performance and allows stakeholders to explore different business areas through interactive reports.

It demonstrates how raw business data can be transformed into meaningful visual insights to support data-driven decision-making.

## 💡 Key Learning Outcomes

- Data cleaning and transformation using Power Query.
- Creating relationships and building data models.
- Writing DAX measures for business analysis.
- Designing multi-page interactive dashboards.
- Implementing Drillthrough for detailed analysis.
- Understanding business requirements and stakeholder needs.

## 🎤 Interview Explanation

**Interviewer: Tell me about your Flipkart Sales Analytics Dashboard.**

**Answer:**

"I developed an interactive Sales Analytics Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI to analyze e-commerce business performance.

The main objective was to provide a centralized view of sales, orders, products, and customer information.

I used Power Query for data cleaning and transformation, created data models, and developed DAX measures for business calculations.

I designed multiple dashboard pages to help different stakeholders explore business performance. I also implemented Drillthrough functionality to provide detailed product-level information.

Through this project, I gained practical experience in data analysis, data modelling, DAX, and business-focused dashboard development."
